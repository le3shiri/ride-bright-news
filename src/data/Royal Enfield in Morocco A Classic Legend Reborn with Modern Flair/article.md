# Royal Enfield in Morocco: A Classic Legend Reborn with Modern Flair

![](images/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-03%20at%2001.31.52.jpeg)

The Moroccan biking scene is experiencing a massive boom, and with it, riders are increasingly seeking the perfect blend of heritage, raw power, and modern reliability. During the recent motorcycle gathering organized by the All Riding Family Association, we caught up with Mohamed, the commercial representative for Royal Enfield Morocco. In an exclusive interview, he walked us through the brand's latest lineup hitting Moroccan roads and their strategy to meet the evolving demands of local "Motards."

A Diverse Lineup Built for Every Rider

Royal Enfield has carved out a unique niche globally and in Morocco by preserving vintage aesthetics while packing modern performance under the hood. During the event, Mohamed showcased the brand’s flagship models currently turning heads in the Moroccan market:

* The Adventure & Off-Road Segment: Led by the globally acclaimed Himalayan and the newly introduced Guerrilla 450, these bikes are engineered from the ground up for long-distance tourers and trail blazers looking to explore Morocco’s rugged terrain.
* The Heavy-Hitting Cruisers & Scramblers: Featuring the rugged Bear 650 and the highway-loving Super Meteor 650—a twin-cylinder beast designed for maximum comfort on endless straights.
* The Urban Daily Drivers: The highly maneuverable 350cc lineup, starring the nimble Hunter 350, which effortlessly blends lightweight handling with a distinct retro-modern look for city commuting.

![](images/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-03%20at%2001.31.52%20(1).jpeg)

Technology & Safety: A Zero-Compromise Approach

Speaking to the community, Mohamed emphasized that bridging the gap between classic styling and modern safety technology is at the very core of Royal Enfield's current engineering philosophy:

"Every single bike in the Royal Enfield lineup—whether it's a 350cc, 450cc, or the 650cc twin-cylinders—comes standard with an advanced ABS (Anti-lock Braking System) developed by Bosch. This ensures maximum traction and flawless stopping power under unpredictable road conditions."

![](images/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-03%20at%2001.31.52%20(2).jpeg)

For off-road purists who want to slide through gravel or sand, Mohamed highlighted a crucial technical feature on the Himalayan: it comes equipped with Switchable ABS. This allows riders to deactivate the ABS on the rear wheel, delivering pure manual control and better handling over loose dirt trails.

![](images/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-03%20at%2001.31.52%20(3).jpeg)

Cultivating a "Safety-First" Biking Culture

Royal Enfield's presence at the All Riding Family Association meet wasn't just about showing off shiny chrome and roaring engines; it was heavily focused on rider education and road safety. Mohamed strongly reminded the community that proper protective gear (helmet, armored jacket, and gloves) paired with a strict respect for traffic laws are the absolute pillars of sustainable motorcycling.

![](images/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-03%20at%2001.31.52%20(4).jpeg)

Furthermore, he noted that regional events outside of the main Casablanca headquarters—like those bringing riders together in El Jadida or Marrakech—provide a golden opportunity for the brand to deliver direct, face-to-face technical support and expert advice to the community.
